A few useful answers

Who can use Clinic Solution?

Doctors, reception staff, pharmacy teams and clinic owners can use the product, with access based on their assigned clinic roles.

Can I use it for more than one clinic?

Yes. Clinic Solution supports multiple clinics with clinic-specific membership and role-based access.

How do I get started?

Create an account to request access for your clinic. If you are already a member, log in to open your workspace.
