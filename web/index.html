Who is Clinic Solution for?

Doctors, reception teams, pharmacy staff and clinic owners who want to manage the day-to-day work of a clinic in one connected product.

Can I use it for more than one clinic?

Yes. Clinic Solution supports multiple clinics with clinic-specific membership and role-based access.

Does it support my team’s language?

The application includes English, Hindi and Gujarati interfaces. You can select a language within the app.

Can I send appointment messages to patients?

Clinic SMS and email workflows and editable templates are available. Delivery requires a configured messaging provider, approved sender/templates where applicable, and may have usage charges.

I already have an account. Where do I sign in?

Log in here to open your existing clinic workspace. New users can create an account.
